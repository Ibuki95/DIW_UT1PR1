Persona 5 Royal

What's Persona 5 Royal?

Persona 5 Royal is the expanded version of Persona 5, a Japanese role-playing game (JRPG) developed by Atlus. It combines two halves: the daily life of a student in Tokyo and the exploration of dungeons called Palaces.

By day, you attend class, work part-time jobs, make friends and build your skills. By night, you enter the Metaverse with your classmates, the Phantom Thieves, to change the hearts of corrupt adults.

The Royal edition adds a new companion, new areas, a third semester with its own story, and many gameplay improvements over the original.

Prologue

You are a high school student with a criminal record for defending a woman. You are sent to Tokyo and placed under the care of a café owner, at a new school where nobody trusts you.

Soon you discover a mysterious app that takes you to a world warped by other people's desires. There, you awaken your Persona and begin living a double life.

Battle System

Battles are turn-based and reward players who learn each enemy's weaknesses.

  • Weaknesses: hit an enemy with the right element to knock it down and earn an extra turn.
  • Baton Pass: hand your turn to an ally with a damage bonus.
  • All-Out Attack: when every enemy is down, the whole team attacks at once.
  • Negotiation: talk to enemies to get items, money or new Personas.
