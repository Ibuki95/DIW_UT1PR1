Other Games

Liked Persona 5 Royal? Here are more games from ATLUS worth playing next.

The Persona Series

  • Persona 3 Reload

    A modern remake of the 2006 classic, about a group of students who fight in a hidden hour of the night.

  • Persona 4 Revival

    A murder mystery in a quiet countryside town, with one of the most loved casts in the series.

  • Persona 5 Strikers

    An action-focused sequel that reunites the Phantom Thieves for a new summer adventure.

Shin Megami Tensei

  • Shin Megami Tensei V: Vengeance

    A dark, demon-filled RPG set in a ruined Tokyo, where you recruit demons and choose your own path.

New Worlds

  • Metaphor: ReFantazio

    A fantasy RPG from the Persona team, with a new world, new mechanics and a strong political story.

  • Unicorn Overlord

    A tactical fantasy RPG with large armies and beautifully painted visuals.

  • 13 Sentinels: Aegis Rim

    A science-fiction story told from the point of view of thirteen teenagers, mixing visual novel and real-time strategy.

More from ATLUS

  • Catherine: Full Body

    A puzzle-adventure game about love, commitment and nightmares.

  • Etrian Odyssey II

    A classic dungeon-crawling series about exploring, mapping and surviving a huge labyrinth.
