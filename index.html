Persona 5 Royal

What's Persona 5 Royal?

Persona is a RPG series set in modern-day Japan that is acclaimed for its narrative style of confronting everyday realities such as forging friendships and romances, alongside supernatural and paranormal themes, including uncovering and solving mysterious rumors and various urban legends.

The series typically follows a story that unfolds after a group of teenagers discover their abilities as Persona-users, chronicling the growth of their friendship and inner selves as they confront numerous obstacles. In recent years, the franchise has been adapted to animated TV series, animated films, music concerts, theatrical stages, manga, and continues to expand its influence.

“Persona” refers to the alternate persona that lies within you—another “you” who materializes themselves in form of mythical deities and demons possessing great power.

Prologue

You are a high school student with a criminal record for defending a woman. You are sent to Tokyo and placed under the care of a café owner, at a new school where nobody trusts you.

Soon you discover a mysterious app that takes you to a world warped by other people's desires. There, you awaken your Persona and begin living a double life.

Game Features

  • Turn-based battles: exploit enemy weaknesses to earn extra turns and chain powerful team attacks.
  • School life: attend class, study for exams and take part in events throughout the school year.
  • Bonds with others: spend time with your friends and allies to grow closer and unlock new abilities.
  • Palaces: sneak through distorted dungeons, avoid enemies and steal the treasure at the end.
  • Personas: collect, fuse and level up the powers you awaken in the Metaverse.
  • Personal growth: raise your Knowledge, Guts, Proficiency, Kindness and Charm with jobs, hobbies and free time.
