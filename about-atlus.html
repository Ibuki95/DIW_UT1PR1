About ATLUS

The studio behind Persona 5 Royal and one of the most distinctive names in Japanese role-playing games.

Who Is ATLUS?

ATLUS is a Japanese video game developer and publisher founded in 1986. Over the decades it has built a strong reputation for role-playing games with dark themes, memorable music and a bold, stylish presentation.

Today the company is part of the SEGA group, and its games are released around the world, including in English-speaking markets.

A Brief History

ATLUS started out making a wide range of games and gradually became known for the Megami Tensei franchise, a long-running series about demons, mythology and difficult moral choices.

In 1996, the first Persona game appeared as a spin-off of that franchise. The series grew into a hit of its own, combining high school life with turn-based combat, and reached a new peak of popularity with Persona 5.

Persona 5 Royal, the expanded edition, was released in Japan in 2019 and worldwide in 2020.

Series ATLUS Is Known For

  • Persona

    Stories about teenagers, social bonds and the power of the mind, mixed with turn-based battles.

  • Shin Megami Tensei

    The older and darker parent series, set in post-apocalyptic worlds full of demons.

  • Etrian Odyssey

    A classic dungeon-crawling series where you draw your own maps.

  • Catherine

    A puzzle-adventure game about relationships, with a strong narrative focus.

The ATLUS Style

ATLUS games are easy to recognize. They tend to feature strong visual identity, jazzy and rock-inspired soundtracks, and stories that deal with real-life themes such as pressure, identity and freedom.

Persona 5 Royal is a clear example of this: a stylish interface, bold colors and a cast of characters who rebel against an unfair society.
